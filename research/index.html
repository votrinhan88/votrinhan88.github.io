Research

My research explores knowledge distillation for vision and language models, focusing on efficient model compression and practical deployment. I'm also interested in adjacent and emerging areas like agentic AI and world models, exploring how these approaches advance our understanding of efficient, practical AI systems.

Causal-Aware Generative AI — Feb 2026 – Present

Generated privacy-preserving synthetic tabular data that preserves the real data’s underlying cause-effect relationships, critical for regulated domains like healthcare and finance

  • Helped design the causal graph extraction step, using causal discovery algorithms validated for robustness across 4 different causal discovery methods
  • Built the core WGAN-GP codebase, including a reinforcement learning (policy-gradient) approach to optimize a non-differentiable structural alignment metric during training
  • Outperformed 7 state-of-the-art baselines across 14 datasets on all four evaluation axes: causal fidelity, downstream utility (F1 nearly matching real-data performance), privacy (lowest re-identification risk), and data quality
  • Ran 5–12x faster than LLM-based competitor methods, completing the full pipeline in ~11 minutes
  • Co-authored 1 paper accepted at ACML 2026

Text Dataset Distillation — 2024–2026

Compressed text training data to 0.1% of its original size while preserving task performance, enabling faster iteration and reduced footprint when working with costly, sensitive, or large-scale text corpora

  • Identified a root-cause failure mode in existing compression methods, where noisy training samples overwhelmed useful ones, and corrected it with a theoretically grounded fix
  • Fine-tuned and benchmarked language models (GPT-2, Llama, Qwen, Gemma; 0.3–4B parameters, 4–32 bit precision) using LoRA, reflecting resource-constrained deployment
  • Engineered the pipeline (TAKE) with plug-and-play extensibility gates, enabling adaptation to new datasets (via additional LoRA fine-tuning) or new objectives (via auxiliary Optimal Transport terms)
  • Validated across 6 benchmarks, compressing to just 0.1% or 20 samples/class at only 5–20% accuracy loss (1-5% better than previous methods), with results transferring cleanly across downstream architectures (Logistic Regression, TextRNN, TextCNN, BERT) and embedding models (TF-IDF, GloVe, MiniLM)
  • Distilled a full dataset in under 10 minutes (distillation step, single V100 GPU), producing human-readable, auditable results
  • First-authored 1 paper accepted at ECML-PKDD 2026

Knowledge Distillation for Black-box Vision Models — 2022–2025

Distilled compact student models under two increasingly restrictive real-world constraints, limited data (<10% available) and no data at all, relevant to deploying models when training data or the source teacher is proprietary, private, or otherwise inaccessible

  • Designed an adaptive GAN training scheme (DivBFKD) that selects high-confidence synthetic images on-the-fly under teacher supervision, improving distillation-set diversity in the few-shot setting; achieved SOTA student accuracy across 7 benchmarks, 1–5% over prior methods
  • Designed a three-phase data-free pipeline (DIPKD) (image prior synthesis → contrastive diversity maximization → a novel “primer student” recovering soft-probability supervision from hard labels), achieving SOTA accuracy across 12 benchmarks, including healthcare imaging to validate domain-specific generalization, by 1–8%
  • Benchmarked across a wide range of CNN architectures (ResNet, VGG), generative model families (GANs, diffusion, flow models), and datasets (including Tiny ImageNet, MedMNIST); full pipeline runtime ranged 2–48 hours depending on model and dataset scale
  • Compressed student models to just 4% of teacher model size while preserving 70–90% of teacher performance
  • First-authored 2 papers accepted at ECML-PKDD 2024 and ICCCI 2026

Detection of COVID-19 from Chest X-rays — 2020–2022

Built an end-to-end deep learning pipeline to screen COVID-19 from chest X-rays, offering a fast, low-cost alternative to PCR testing suitable for resource-constrained or high-throughput clinical settings

  • Designed a full training pipeline (data augmentation, Bayesian hyperparameter optimization with k-fold cross-validation, early stopping, threshold tuning) specifically to handle severe class imbalance (27:1 negative-to-positive ratio) common in real-world medical datasets
  • Benchmarked three deployment-relevant model sizes — from a large, high-accuracy model (ResNet50) to a sub-6M-parameter lightweight model (NASNet-A-Mobile) — enabling a choice between desktop-grade accuracy and mobile-deployable footprint for field or resource-constrained clinical use
  • Tuned decision thresholds to prioritize recall (98% sensitivity), the clinically appropriate trade-off for a screening tool where missed cases are costlier than false alarms
  • Achieved AU-ROC up to 0.996 and AU-PRC up to 0.977 at high throughput (100s of images/s), outperforming multiple prior published methods on the same public benchmark
  • First-authored 1 paper published in Health Informatics Journal (2024)
